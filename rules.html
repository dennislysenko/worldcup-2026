How many flags? — the selection rule

The icon is chosen from the slot's probability distribution, so its shape matches reality.

1 · How many (N) — cliff-aware: always show #1. Add #2 if it's ≥ 15%. Then add #3, #4 only while each is ≥ 70% of the team above it — so a big drop-off (a “cliff”) stops the list.
2 · Which style: N=1 → single · N=2 & gap ≤ 6pts → even pair · N=2 & gap > 6pts → cascade · N=3 → tri-shield · N=4 → grid.
So 42/30/18/10 stops at 2 (18 is <70% of 30 — past the cliff) → leader of two, not a misleading trident.
